Hyderabadi Mutton Biryani

Hyderabadi Mutton Biryani is a fragrant and spicy rice dish made with tender marinated mutton, basmati rice, and aromatic spices, slow-cooked to perfection. 🌿🍖 It’s a royal delicacy from Hyderabad, known for its rich flavors and irresistible taste.

Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani

Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani is a flavorful blend of juicy marinated chicken, long-grain basmati rice, and a medley of spices, cooked in the traditional dum style. 🍗✨ It’s one of Hyderabad’s most famous dishes, loved for its rich aroma and taste.
